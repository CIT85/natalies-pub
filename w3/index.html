What is Magic: The Gathering?

Magic: The Gathering is a popular trading card game where players build decks and battle against each other. Players use creatures, spells, and lands to defeat their opponents.

My Favorite Format

My favorite format is Commander because players can build decks with 100 cards and choose a legendary creature to lead their deck.
